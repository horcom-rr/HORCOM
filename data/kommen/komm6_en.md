# Notes on the Münchner Rhythmenlehre

*Robert Rettig, from the original HORCOM documentation (KOMMEN7P folder), English edition, his wording preserved in translation.*

DÖBEREINER TRIGGERING: Determines the moments of the rhythmic triggerings after WOLFGANG DÖBEREINER.

You can choose between a GRAPHIC output, a TABLE output with PLANETS, and a DEGREE-DATE TABLE.

Abbreviations:

- LJ = year of life
- MO = month, decimal
- PL = triggered planet
- ART = type of triggering:
  - D: DIRECT triggering of the relevant planet by "running over" it.
  - P: triggering as "PHASE RULER"
  - P2/P3: triggering of the possible 2nd/3rd phase ruler.

In the graphic, the upward-pointing arrow stands for the clockwise direction = "RIGHT", the downward-pointing one for the opposite direction = "LEFT".

In the TABLE, beneath the PHASE RULER or directly triggered planet stand the planets triggered indirectly via ASPECT or MIRRORING at the cardinal axes, with the corresponding aspect symbol, marked with "S" in the case of mirroring.

With the date output the program also outputs the days, which are however to be understood only as reference points.

With SEPTARS please always choose the date output, since the LJ/MO output is too coarse here!!

The order in the TABLE corresponds to the sequence of "being triggered" during the pass through the horoscope, whereby the phase ruler or rulers come first. The order of the planets corresponds to the position table in the EPHEMERIDE COORDINATES table.

Lunar nodes (DR, DS), AS, MC and the cardinal points AR, CN, LI, CP (0 AR, 0 CN and so on) are counted along as "planets" in this sequence. With CARDINAL POINTS, however, only the DIRECT triggerings are registered.

In the GRAPHIC (= HOROSCOPE) representation, on the left of the picture is the time axis (from bottom to top) for ONE PHASE. To the left of this axis the direct and phase triggerings can be seen, to the right of it the planets triggered indirectly via aspect or mirroring (S). At the far right the triggering planet is also noted, with the aspect symbol. The phase ruler (or rulers) is (are) shown INVERTED, unless it is at the same time encountered directly.

The current phase is marked by an encircling red arc. In addition to the aforementioned points, a SPECIAL POINT can be defined, either as a TIME point or as a specific DEGREE. This point is marked by * or by "F", the exact degree value given below the horoscope.

ADVANCE from PHASE to PHASE with the SPACE BAR!!

From the SEPTAR or SOLAR level you can run through the triggerings with various phases. This always concerns the same RADIX horoscope. If you want to change this, you first have to go back to the RADIX level and run SEPTAR or SOLAR again with new conditions.

Besides the planets, a "SPECIAL POINT" can also be defined, for example one of the "group-fate degrees" with particular qualities that W. DÖBEREINER discovered, for example 17.5 degrees Capricorn, with the characteristic Mars-Saturn. This special point is, as otherwise a "fixed point", marked with a red "F". Besides the degree entry, this point can also be assigned to a specific date.

To avoid confusion, any other fixed point that may be defined is switched off in the outputs for the M.R.

From all TABLE outputs you can, by pressing the FUNCTION KEY F2, look at the respective horoscope in between and go back again with 'Esc'.

With the DEGREE-DATE LIST the HALF ECLIPTIC degrees and the associated date are each listed.

This list can be represented both ecliptically and MUNDANE. Setting under "HOROSCOPE PRESELECTION... first line!".

The "group-fate degrees" already published by W. DÖBEREINER are each entered in a small box with the corresponding planet symbols.

The CARDINAL points are marked red.

You can also DEFINE FURTHER degrees YOURSELF for research purposes. You are then made aware whether the point already exists in the M.R. or not. Please do not pass on your own definitions as MÜNCHNER RHYTHMENLEHRE! These degrees are marked by red symbols. For the additionally entered degrees the mirror point at 0° Aries-Libra, with the same characteristic, is also entered.

CAUTION! The order in the list has DEGREE or SIGN PRIORITY, so that at the transition from Pisces to Aries a jump occurs in the sorting, whose avoidance would be somewhat elaborate.

The triggering dates in the MUNDANE representation naturally deviate from the ecliptically calculated ones. The results according to the MUNDANE geometry are to be presumed more exact.

The differences can well amount to several months. They are relatively small for house pictures in which the ecliptic main axes are approximately perpendicular to one another.

The mundane horoscope is selected under "HOROSCOPE PRESETS" in the first line and, by the way, also offers a meaningful connection with the topic of the "PRIMARY DIRECTIONS".

See also Notes 4 and 7 on this.

If you want to go BACKWARD in time, enter a NEGATIVE value at the prompt "Period per house", for example instead of 7 years -7 years.

An important possibility is presented by working with the "MUNDANE" HOROSCOPE, which for the M.R. is likely to bring more accurate results than working with the normal, ecliptic horoscope. In the mundane horoscope one always works, namely, with houses equal in time and space of 30 degrees each on the EQUATOR, which correspond to the PLACIDUS HOUSES of the ecliptic. The "mundane" positions of the houses and planets can therefore, with full justification, be converted as simple time proportions, while in the ecliptic representation this usually holds only approximately. Larger errors arise here especially at higher geographic latitudes or (and) with large ecliptic latitude (for example with PL, MO, ME).

In extreme cases a planet may thereby have to be assigned to a different house, or be shifted by several months in the triggering. To these circumstances, as far as I know, E.R. DOSTAL first drew attention, a student of W. DÖBEREINER, for many years a lecturer at the latter's Munich evening courses, who is also known among M.R. adherents through his astrology pocket calculator HORUS.

To all practicing M.R. adherents, working with the MUNDANE horoscope is therefore warmly recommended. Your experiences with it would also interest me very much. Please write to me about it occasionally. Somewhat confusing will perhaps be that no aspect lines are entered in the mundane horoscope. The reason for this is that all aspects, mirrorings and so on are still referred to the ECLIPTIC and only the triggering moments are calculated in the equatorial geometry. For detailed studies it is recommended to use both representations (I already sense the suggestions for improvement).

The group-fate degrees in equatorial measure likewise shift, though usually only within half a degree.

In the representation of the MUNDANE HOROSCOPE the ecliptic sign sectors have been retained, which are then, however, mostly more or less distorted.
