# Notes on Houses

*Robert Rettig, from the original HORCOM documentation (KOMMEN7P folder), English edition, his wording preserved in translation.*

"HOUSE SYSTEM" serves to change the preselected house system. "HOUSE TABLE" shows the house cusps of the preselected system. A certain special position is held by the TOPOCENTRIC system, which also works beyond the polar circles, in contrast to the PLACIDUS system, but otherwise up to about +-55 degrees latitude delivers almost the same values as the latter (deviation mostly <20'). This system was proposed by POLICH and PAGE.

A description of the system can be found in: G. DEAN "RECENT ADVANCES IN NATAL ASTROLOGY". The formulas, also those of the other systems, are taken from there.

In the HOUSE calculation the NUTATION of the Earth's axis is always taken into account, which yields the so-called "TRUE SIDEREAL TIME".

In older publications the MEAN sidereal time (without nutation) was mostly used, to a good extent still today as well. Astrologically correct is without doubt the TRUE value.

Newly added are the EQUAL ECLIPTIC HOUSES, also in the variant after VEHLOW. These two systems affect only the normal horoscope and double horoscope and ecliptic directions. Otherwise the system switches to PLACIDUS where applicable, in particular with COMPOSIT, COMBIN and equatorial directions.

EQUAL EQUATORIAL HOUSES occur only in connection with the preselection "MUNDANE HOROSCOPE".

Besides the house cusps, the VERTEX frequently mentioned in the American literature is also given (= line of intersection between ECLIPTIC and 1st VERTICAL). The 1st vertical is the great circle through the zenith that stands perpendicular to the meridian circle. It passes through the east and west points of the horizon. (Formula from G. DEAN..).

If AC and the first house, or MC and the 10th house, do not coincide (with equal houses), the first is labeled H1, the 10th H10.
