# Code of conduct

HORCOM keeps the life's work of Robert Rettig alive. Everyone who takes part should feel welcome, whatever their background or experience, and whether they practise astrology or simply care about the program.

## What we ask

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to open source or to astrology.
- Assume good intent, and keep criticism about the work, not the person.
- Respect privacy. Never share someone's birth data or other personal details without their consent.

## What is not accepted

- Insults, harassment, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Mocking someone for their beliefs or for practising astrology.
- Publishing someone's private information.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, please write to the maintainer by email, the address is on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/schwimmbeck). Reports are treated confidentially.

## What happens

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close discussions and block people who don't follow this code. It applies to the issues, pull requests and discussions of this repository, and to anyone speaking for the project elsewhere.
