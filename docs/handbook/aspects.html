Purpose

Robert's aspect engine is a harmonic scanner. It walks the divisors of the circle from one to twelve, or sixteen, and tests every body pair against a window around each multiple of the base angle. A pair that matched under a smaller divisor is claimed and never tested again, so the classical aspect always wins over an exotic one. The same machinery, run three times with different offsets, finds the midpoint contacts.

The orb system

The default window of a divisor is a thirtieth of its base angle scaled by the global ORBIS-FAKTOR, twelve degrees for the conjunction, six for the opposition, four for the trine. Each body carries a percentage weight, the pair takes the LARGER of the two weights, and a weight of zero silences a body entirely. His alternative mode, the gleichwahrscheinliche Aspekte, swaps in a user table of orbs and restricts each divisor to its classical multiples, twelve keeps only 1, 5, 7 and 11, eight keeps the odd ones. His dialog explains the intent in his own words

...werden Orbes gewählt, die etwa GLEICHE Wahrscheinlichkeit für alle Aspekte mit Teiler 1 bis 12 aufweisen ! ( 1930 bis 2050 )

The Schiemenz counters

Two statistics ride inside the scanner, both introduced for the research correspondence his change log attributes to Schiemenz. The Triga counter joins conjunctions that share a body, consuming the shared ends so nothing counts twice. The grand trine counter searches triples of divisor three hits whose endpoints close a triangle, with a degeneracy guard against three bodies standing in one spot.

Midpoints

Three passes, direct, square and the semi square family, test every body against the half sum of every pair, each with the midpoint's antipode, one degree of base orb. A shared cube of flags spans the passes so the square that also fits the semi square family counts once. Two quirks are kept knowingly, the third body's weight only gates positivity in orbis_discr3, and a body standing at exactly zero degrees Aries never aspects at all because the original tests the raw position against zero. The tests document both.

Verification
