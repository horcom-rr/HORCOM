chart

The pipeline, the angles, the houses and the protected parallax

handbook / chart

Purpose

This module turns an epoch and a place into a chart. It is the C++ form of the original chain, jseckp for the angles, a60 for the houses, a9 with a90 for the bodies. Houses run in Universal Time, the bodies run in Ephemeris Time, and every body passes the correction chain par_ap_ktr, light time, optionally aberration, conversion to equatorial, and Robert Rettig's topocentric parallax.

The shell also carries the table screens of the original around the chart, the Grad-Liste as the sorted degree list of every point with the intermediate houses on demand, the Häuser-Tabelle with each cusp's longitude, right ascension and declination and under Placidus the oblique ascension with its pole height, the Großes Jahr with the precession driven age point over a chosen reference epoch, and a small conversions dialog for the julian date, delta T, local time and the degree minute second split.

The two clocks of the original

One faithful quirk deserves its own paragraph. The original computes the sidereal time by rewinding to 0h UT and running the full juld chain there, which leaves the nutation state of MIDNIGHT behind in the globals. The angles and the house cusps therefore see the true obliquity of 0h UT, while the planets, computed later in Ephemeris Time, see the obliquity of the epoch. The difference is tiny and entirely his, the pipeline reproduces it through the ekls0 field.

The protected parallax

The parallax cluster is ported verbatim under the CLAUDE working rule that presumes the original right. His comment names the source and the simplification

// Parallaxe //aus Montenbruck S.25 Ohne Ber. d.Meereshöhe ro = 0.000042634515 //Erdradius in AE
the head of the original procedure par

The formulation looks unusual, the declination arm reuses the y component of the displaced vector through a sine, but the algebra works out exactly, z / n equals z' / sqrt(x'² + y'²) of the standard vector method on his spherical Earth. The test suite exploits that, an independent cartesian computation must agree with his route to machine precision, and it does. The parallax applies to slots 1 through 10 only, nodes and extra bodies stay geocentric, exactly like the SELECT in the original.

Bodies and speeds

The Sun comes from the Earth's VSOP place reversed, the Moon from the series with no light time, planets pass helio_to_geo whose daily motion is a symmetric difference at a tenth of the daily rates. Pluto prefers his integrated file and falls back to Chapront outside it. Extra bodies read their .eph files or solve their Kepler orbits, with rates from a symmetric hundredth of a day like the original pl_vel. The Part of Fortune follows ta_na, day birth AC plus Moon minus Sun, night birth the mirror. True node and true Black Moon get their speeds from a symmetric hour, the original vel_om_pd.

The exact hit search

plant answers when a body reaches a given longitude, searching backward from a start moment. The port keeps his whole scheme, the per body initial steps and tolerances from one degree for Mercury down to 1.6 minutes for Pluto, the period table of a16_ta, the coarse backward walk with the branch reconcilers ver and ver1r until the target is bracketed, the fine parity guarded approach of the planets, then the damped secant that multiplies the step by minus 0.31 on every sign flip, and the final linear interpolation. His screen escapes and timers become step budgets. The original notes the remaining time error is mostly under five seconds. Verified against the 1993 spring equinox of the almanac, 14:41 UT, hit within two minutes, with the residual under half an arc second, plus a lunar return and a Mercury search near its retrograde loops. The solar and lunar returns already ride on it like korh, the solar seeded at the birthday clock of the target year plus fifteen days, the lunar found as the return preceding a chosen moment at 0h UT.

The transit sweep

a180 walks a time window in body sized intervals and tests every running body against every radix target shifted by each multiple of the base angle. The port keeps his interval table, his bracket tests with the branch reconcilers, the fifteen degree speed cap per interval, and the stationary guard that flags a slow body touching a target, remembered once per pair like his c3 memory. Two deliberate deviations, each an improvement on the same data. The direction of the bracket follows the true daily motion, so the wrap of the circle is no longer mistaken for a backward arc, and every bracketed hit is refined to the exact moment with the plant search instead of his linear interpolation, a hit that fails to refine into its interval is dropped rather than guessed. The moon stays filtered by the cap unless the moon flag opens it, matching his default. Verified over November 1992 against the 1992 radix, every event sits on its target to under an arc second and the sun finds radix venus on the fourteenth. The ingress driver ingre1 rides the same search, twelve sign entries per body with the epsilon that keeps the zero longitude guard quiet and the sun corrected into the calendar year of the start, the 1993 table pinned sign by sign. The multi passage continuation of plant comes next.

Composite and combin

The composite midpoints two charts body by body with the near side rule of halbsmin including its far side tie, the south node follows the north by half a circle, and the AC and MC midpoints flip to the side of their cusps. The houses follow his three modes, the mean of sidereal time, longitude and latitude, the Robert Hand armc from the MC midpoint at a residence, or the schematic halbsummen from the MC midpoint with his ordering chain that keeps every cusp ahead of its neighbour. A composite has no latitudes or speeds and the port leaves them at zero. The combin of a14 simply averages moment and place and runs the normal pipeline. In the shell both live in the Horoskop menu over the same partner as the comparison, the house mode follows his profile flags.

The directed axes

prima turns the radix ARMC at his Naibod style rate, three hundred sixty degrees per tropical year of elapsed days over the year length, direct or converse, and recomputes the axes and cusps from the directed ARMC at the chosen place, drawn slightly smaller over the radix positions with the STZ difference in the centre and no chords. His sidereal time variation rides on the same turn, one degree being four clock minutes of birth time, which made this screen his rectification tool. The port keeps the rate, the variation and the display, the interactive summing of test variations stays with the operator through repeated runs.

The mundane mode

horm 2 leaves the ecliptic. Every body converts to equatorial coordinates, md11 places it east or west of the meridian axis, and md projects it along its semi arc, ninety degrees plus or minus the ascensional difference asin of tan latitude times tan declination, into oblique ascension under its own quadrant. The house space longitude then counts from the ascendant at zero and the cusps become the exact equal grid of mundhorh. All scanners run unchanged on the converted values, exactly the original's trick of feeding mup into the same aspect machinery. Pinned on a flat sky where the proportion turns linear, quadrant by quadrant, and on a real chart whose grid and ascendant land exactly.

The heliocentric mode

hrg turns the whole pipeline sun centred. Every planet, ephemeris body and Kepler body simply keeps its heliocentric spherical state, ecliptic longitude, latitude and the sun distance, converted to right ascension and declination but never through the geocentric correction chain, no aberration, no light time, no parallax. The Earth takes the Moon's slot carrying the VSOP Earth itself, the Sun's slot stays empty, and the geocentric ideas leave with it, no Moon, no nodes, no Black Moon, no Part of Fortune. Houses do not exist on a sun centred sky, horg11 and bes111 stayed dark in the original and the port computes none, the wheel anchors zero Aries on the left like horbeg clearing fza and marks the Sun as the small circle in the centre that horg1 drew, the Earth wearing the crossed circle glyph on the ring. The aspect scanner runs unchanged over whatever stands on the slots, exactly his trick of reusing one machinery for every mode.

The return family

Beyond solar and lunar, the a16 flow returns every body. A planetar is a body's return onto its own radix longitude, the Nth return seeded a safe head start past the crossing and then found by the backward search, with the count stamped in front of the name just as solnummer did. A personar is the sun crossing the radix position of a chosen body within the first year of life. A septar is the solar return of the year of life whose seven year phase covers the chosen age, the rhythm the Döbereiner school reads in periods of seven, the first septar being the radix itself. In heliocentric mode the solar becomes the terrar, the Earth's return.

Progressions and the day chart

The secondary progression of proho maps one day of sky motion onto one tropical year of life. Four clock answers decide the moment within the progressed day, the birth clock kept, the birth's true solar time found again by the damped search of wahrso, the houses turned at one day per year through the sidereal ladder of hd_hs, or the julian date interpolated strictly. The day chart of taho finds on any chosen day the moment whose sun stands at the birth's hour angle, the same true solar time that ruled the first breath.

Symbolic, primary and arc directions

The direction evaluations turn arcs into ages. The symbolic methods measure how far one radix point stands from another's aspect grid, in ecliptic longitude, in right ascension or in mundane house space, and the classic key of one degree per year names the age, direct or converse by the arc's side, every arc listed once since it reads the same from both ends. The primary directions of the E.C. Kühr school measure the same idea in oblique ascension, every significator under the pole its semi arc proportion hands it, the aspect points of the promissors carrying a latitude that fades with the angle. The secondary direction list runs the transit machinery on the day for a year axis and stretches the found dates back into life, and the sun and moon arc lists use the fact that a rigid arc moves all points equally, so every contact is a crossing of the light alone over shifted targets. All of them meet in one evaluation dialog.

The multiple directions

The MULTI menu directs the whole chart by age. MULTI 1 advances every body by its age times its degree within the sign, MULTI 2 by age times the whole longitude, MULTI 3 runs the same sign referred rate from a chosen reference point, a body, an angle, a cusp, the classic ruler of a house or a sign start. The zero point pairs anchor every body on the start of its own rulership sign, the day signs eastward, the night signs westward, and MULTI-ARC scales the arc between reference and body. The axes answer the haus_ber question like the harmonics, and under the new MC answer a901_m rebuilds the Part of Fortune from the directed lights. Transpluto stays dark across the whole MULTI world.

Harmonics and the 90 degree circle

A harmonic multiplies every longitude onto an integer order, harm21's world. The south node follows the transformed north node by half a circle rather than turning itself, which differs for even orders, Transpluto drops out exactly as his CASE list leaves it, and the Part of Fortune multiplies like every other extra, harm21 keeps its a901_m rebuild commented out. Houses answer his haus_ber question, treated like bodies every cusp multiplies, or the new MC derives a fresh ARMC and the whole house system recomputes at the birth latitude. The 90 degree circle of a12f is the times four special case on the double wheel, the aspect scan runs on the multiplied state including the angles, then the AC and MC glyphs divide back into the first quarter for display exactly like his pl(13) line. The shell offers both from the Horoskop menu, the harmonic as its own double wheel over the radix and the dial as the second mode of the comparison.

The rhythm school and the Dynamogramm

The Münchner Rhythmenlehre after W. Döbereiner walks the twelve houses as life phases of seven years. Every phase triggers the bodies inside its house, the ruler of its sign and the rulers of intercepted signs, and every trigger pulls in its aspect partners out of the radix matrix and its mirror point partners, each age read from the body's place within its own house, in years or in the month unit. The Dynamogramm after Krafft and Goerner runs the progressed sky at one day per year, finds every orb passage against the radix and among the running points, and spans each with a cosine half wave or a bell curve, signed by his valuation scheme of the pairs and angles, weighted by the running point's amplitude, summed into the ground mood and the existential line of the angles over a five year window. The original carried these as its richest interactive screens, the ring graphic of the trigger walk and the printed arc pictures stay behind, the tables and the summed curves carry the substance.

Fixed stars and arabic parts

The star screen carries his own catalogue of sixty two entries, each with the planetary quality the tradition assigns and the distance in light years. Positions move linearly from the millennium epoch, the Pleiades, Hyades and Praesepe clusters and the galactic centre and apex precess properly from their own epochs, annual aberration rides on the chart's sun, and every star is matched against the chart with his narrowing windows, two degrees for the conjunction, half of that for the opposition, a quarter for the square, a third for the trine. The arabic parts follow the one formula base plus first minus second, swapped on a night birth, thirty seven built in from the Glückspunkt to the point of death with the contributors he credited by name, the wealth point keeping its additive exception, and the user's own definitions loading from the two ARABTEI files beside the data.
