Purpose

The renderer rebuilds Robert's chart wheel from his exact geometry, the virtual canvas of 640 by 480 with the centre at 430, 224, the scale km 0.95, the rings at 90, 128, 152, 165 and 182, the polar mapping w = nb(lambda + pi − fza) that puts the ascendant on the left, the axes reaching 188 with their labels just outside. The output is a backend neutral display list, one code path can feed the screen, an SVG file or a printer later, which resolves the original's three way moda& branching by design.

The sign band wears the colours as they actually appeared on his screen. His code asked for red, olive, teal and cyan, but the 48 colour palette and the hatch fills of deffi rendered salmon, green, a pale cyan grey and blue, verified against captures of the original running under emulation. The sign glyphs keep the white ground of his SRCCOPY sprite stamps as a small box on the coloured band. The true apogee Lilith and both birth rulers of geb_herr, the ascendant sign ruler and, when a whole sign lies intercepted in the first house, the ruler of that sign, sit inverted on a dark square, the whole sprite cell his SRCINVERT stamping inverted, the same in the aspektarium, the midpoint trees and the rhythm strip. The true node pair carried the same stamp in the original and the builder still offers it, but the shell draws the nodes as normal planets on the wish of the author's family. Retrograde bodies carry his red R, and AC and MC stack the rounded degree in sign below their tag like habes, DC and IC carry the bare tag.

The symbols themselves are his. The original shipped six size variants of every planet, sign and aspect drawing in symbbmp, loaded by Symbhol and stamped by putbm. The rewrite carries the large 24 pixel variants, upscaled with one scale3x pass that straightens single pixel stairs without inventing curves, and draws them wherever the original stamped a sprite. Every sprite is averaged down once to the exact device pixels it lands on and set on the pixel grid together with its ground, the bilinear stretch of the raster engine thinned his strokes when it shrank the large sprite twenty fold. Under each sprite lies a paper cutout so no line runs through a symbol, exactly the erasing his white sprite backgrounds did. Each body also gets the two position marks of plein11, one at the sign band and one on the aspect circle, and the chords follow the ASPECT LINES rows of his aspz1. Every hit maps to a row, the first and last multiple of a divisor to its base row two to twelve, the compound multiples to the rows 13 to 19 from the biquintile to the quincunx, and each row carries a colour and a DEFLINE style. HORCOM's standard draws the opposition, square and quintile solid, the trine, sextile and the higher families dotted and the septiles dash dotted, in red, green, blue and black. His own profile ran the OWN SETTINGS of that screen, the colours and styles he picked per row, and the port starts with them. The conjunction is his red ring of radius 3 at radius 85, the Munich rhythm theory leaves the eighth and twelfth divisors out. Everything asp1 draws needs a divisor above one and the ecliptic frame, the chords, the inner position marks at radius 90, the blue dashed node line and the black dashed line of the Black Moon through the centre, so WITHOUT ASPECT LINES and the mundane frame leave all of them out. bes2 writes the name of the chart into the centre, RADIX, the SOLAR or SEPTAR label, COMPOSITE with its house method under it, 90° DIAL, Mundane under the name in the mundane frame, and nothing on the plain double wheel, the transit, clock, MULTI and direction screens keep their own centre text.

On screen the wheel sits centred on a square sheet, the record notes in the corner, since the coordinate and house tables live in their own panels now. The name rides top-left, the mode top-centre, the sidereal time top-right, and per the tester's v5 mockup the Place block, the moment block and the credit stamp share the bottom-right corner. The paired sheets of Composite and Combine follow the a13aus corners of the original, the first chart stands as a numbered name row in place of the Name: block, the second chart stacks its name and moment as short rows above the bottom-left corner, the Combine adds its COMBINE UT mean there, and no row is wide enough to reach the wheel. A Combine of more than two writes RECORDS: with the slot numbers in place of the name and stacks the names there instead, its Place block names the stored mean place COMBIN-ORT with the mean coordinates. The Composite names its house method only in the centre like bes2_comp and keeps the Place block for the residence of ROBERT HAND, the other modes have no place of their own. With real houses its first cusp reads H1 and the AC midpoint gets a thick axis of its own, his two tags stood on one spot when the midpoint came close to the cusp, the port then stacks the AC tag above H1. The exported sheet of a composite drops the Vel. column and lists the AC row under H1 like bes111. The double wheel itself draws at the original a12 scale km 0.8 (multi and harmonics at their 0.82), so the outer house band and its axis labels stay inside the sheet instead of riding over the corner text. The export paths, SVG, PDF and printing, still produce his classic 640 by 480 sheet, the body table with velocities down the left margin, the house summary, the Mirror: pairs of spieg1 two to a row as MO-CH, and the record corners around the wheel on the right, one page and no separate table sheet. The left panel matches his HOROSCOPE GRAPHIC screen of 7P, the histograms of the older 5P screenshot on his web pages no longer stood there. The GUI hands its tinted sprite cache into the SVG writer as embedded images, so the export carries his symbols like the screen, while the Qt free CLI falls back to font glyphs. Both the SVG export and the command line wheel wear Robert Rettig's own colours and aspect lines, the same as the program window.

His text procedures gave every height a width of their own through textg, seven for the heights up to 14, eight for 15 and 16, ten up to 20 and sixteen up to 30, and stood each text on its bottom line (TA_BOTTOM). Every list and graph screen is laid out on these metrics, a heading centred as (640 - tw * LEN) / 2, a column rule where his cells end. One port of his routines in items.hpp builds the texts of these screens, screen_text for text and textc, screen_text_centred for textzent, screen_text_vertical for the upward texts and add_screen_text_boxed for the framed textzentrl. They take his y as the bottom line and carry his width as the pitch of the primitive.

The pitch is the room his layout gives a character, a ceiling. Where Courier New is wider, as from height 12 on, the face narrows to it. Where it is narrower, as for the small cell texts, it keeps its own advance, since his vector font stretched a height 9 text to seven units and ran the degree of an ASPEKTARIUM cell over the rule. text_advance gives that step. The painter narrows the face to it and closes the rest with a letter spacing, so every character steps exactly one cell whatever pixel size the view rounds to, and the SVG writer sets the same length. Boxes laid out around a text, such as the MULTI midpoint boxes, count with the same advance and hold their text. His windows clipped every drawing to their client area, and the screen and the printed page clip a sheet to its paper the same way, so a DYNAMOGRAMM curve or arc running past the frame ends at the edge instead of on the desk.

Deliberate modernisations, each documented

Three parts of the original drawing cannot carry over literally, and the legacy analysis called all three. The flood fill of the sign band becomes explicit annular sector paths. His bitmap glyphs in six sizes become text glyphs, Unicode astrological symbols where they exist and his two letter tags where they do not, which realises the resolution independent vector symbols he himself prototyped in SYMBGEN. And the tiled screen dump persistence is replaced by real SVG export. The sign band keeps the colour modes of his COLOURS question, hatched standard colours, his own colours hatched or solid, or no fill at all with the sign symbols black or dressed in their element colour. His own profile hatched red, green, cyan and blue, which his screen showed as salmon, green, pale cyan grey and blue, and the band draws exactly those shades. Any other colour takes the ink share of the same hatch pattern, 45 percent for fire, 92 for earth, 13 for air and 73 for water, blended onto white. The aspect chords keep the rows of aspz1 with the blue dashed node axis.

The chart defaults

CHANGE HOROSCOPE DEFAULTS, his avh, sets how the wheel is drawn, and the display list reads every answer. SMALL SYMBOLS shrink the body glyphs to five sevenths, his plsyver half width of 5 against 7, and plentz11 staggers them at seven tenths, the sign glyphs keep their size. DEGREES and RETROGRADES writes the degree under each glyph and the R of a retrograde body beside it, ONLY the DEGREES drops the R, NEITHER OF THEM both, and the mundane frame shows neither like planziff1. The R marks every retrograde planet and extra body, never the nodes, in the colour of the outer symbols like planziff2. The outer symbols themselves wear that colour on the transit, TIME WANDERING and DOUBLE WHEEL rings, MULTI and HARMONICS keep them black. BEGINNING of the CHART turns the wheel like horbeg, the ascendant, the MC, zero Aries or zero Libra on the left, or any degree, the mundane frame and the heliocentric chart keep their own origin. The ASSIGNMENT of SIGN RULERS picks the modern or the old rulers of Scorpio, Aquarius and Pisces for the session, and every reader of his ze_pl follows it, the inverted birth rulers of the wheel, the histograms, the Arabic parts, the rhythm theory and the statistics conditions.

His full sheets

The DOUBLE WHEEL, the 90 DEGREE DIAL, MULTI and HARMONICS show the whole sheet of the original instead of the centred wheel, the double wheel on the right and render/pair_sheet on the left. Two narrow coordinate columns like bes11 and bes111 in their dppel and mult dress, rows nine apart in size nine, "SO 12 AR 34" or with more than one extra "SO 12°AR 34'", the P of the parallax, M or W behind node and Black Moon, the fixed point red, the house cusps AC to H12, only AC and MC on the dial and in the zero point modes. The heliocentric wheel

The hrg mode carried its own look and the port keeps it. horbeg clears fza, so zero Aries anchors the left instead of an ascendant that a sun centred sky does not have, the whole horg11 house block stays dark, and horg1 marks the Sun as the small unscaled circle in the wheel's centre. The Moon's slot wears the crossed circle glyph of the Earth, the swap of pls&(2) for symbol twenty two in plsyls1, and the label line under the date reads Heliocentric like his settings screen.

The 90 degree circle

Under dop 4 the sign band collapses to the three qualities like the zein fill, three sectors wearing the first three element colours with the first sign of each quality as its glyph. Under his own colours the third sector keeps the hatch of air but wears the water colour, as his fill_color took it under dop 4. The dividers land on their shared boundaries, and only AC and MC survive as axes since DC and IC multiply onto them, aeqh blanks their labels. Everything else, rings, glyph ring, de clumping and chords, runs unchanged over the transformed positions.

The de clumping

plentz is ported as a whole, the sort along the wheel, the radial stagger of plus eight or minus fourteen units when neighbours come within ten degrees, the angular push apart within nine degrees with his three different strengths. One honest limit is kept, the original resolves a stellium in one pass and may leave a single touching pair in extreme cases, the port behaves the same and the test asserts the improvement rather than a guarantee the original never made.

The screen view

The screen widget adds what the fixed Atari canvas never could. The mouse wheel zooms around the cursor up to twelve fold, a left drag pans the zoomed sheet, a double click resets the view, and the export and print paths always see the whole sheet regardless of the zoom. The Planet selection carries his right mouse selection over, single bodies red or alone on the ring and the birth ruler highlighted like his inverse glyph. Which aspect lines the wheel draws is set where he set it, in the ASPECT LINES screen of CHANGE HOROSCOPE DEFAULTS.

Printing and PDF

The original asked OUTPUT on the SCREEN or as a PRINTER GRAPHIC and drove a GDI printer context through the same drawing procedures. The port keeps that shape with one shared painter, the primitive loop lives in paint_display_list and feeds the screen widget, the printer and the PDF writer. DIN A5 prints the classic sheet, so whatever the wheel currently shows, radix, transits, a comparison, the 90 degree circle, prints exactly as drawn. DIN A4 is a4_print_sheet, the upright 640 by 980 page of his a11 with the wheel over the bes_big boxes, positions, aspects with the mirror contacts, house cusps and the three midpoint families.

Everything printed goes through his druck_einr_anz first, the box PRINTER READY ?, then his setup box naming the paper and the orientation, HARDCOPY - FORMAT : DIN A5 for a hardcopy, With 'SETUP' and SET UP and WAIT until the PRINTER WORKS !, and then the system print dialog. PRINT… runs the same setup boxes and the same page geometry as the PRINTER GRAPHIC of an output. A print that fails or is cancelled says PRINTER ABORTED like dr_fehl, only the record list returns quietly like his datei path. The pages keep his geometry, robert_page_rect maps the 640 by 459 screen at 0.8 points a unit from a twentieth of the printable width and a ninetieth of its height, about 180.6 by 129.5 mm, the a11 chart graphic starts at a fifteenth of the width, the full page HARDCOPY and the A4 linear graphic grow by 1.4 in landscape, and the a11 A4 page lies at 0.75 points a unit from a fourteenth and an eightieth. Every printed page carries the date and time behind its credit like drad2, a table without a credit gets the dradst line centred at its foot. The screen and the SVG export stay without the stamp so the golden comparisons keep holding.

His PRINTER OPTION rules the rest. His converters, defaults, explanations and the tools of MISCELLANEOUS stay without the box unless a paired chart is up. HOROSCOPE GRAPHIC and the linear graphic ask SCREEN, DIN A5 or DIN A4 first, the three paired charts and the SOLAR and LUNAR tables SCREEN or DIN A5, and the screen answer then waits for a key with the HARDCOPY behind it. The command line front end

apps/cli builds the horcom executable. It takes a date, time in UT, place and the calculation switches, prints the coordinate table with the sign tags of his zei$ table, the houses and the aspect counts, and writes the wheel as SVG on request. The first wheel it produced cross checked against the book, the Sun of 1992-10-13 landed within seconds of arc of the Meeus example advanced by three hours of daily motion.

Verification

test_render.cpp checks that the ascendant label sits on the left horizon, that the list carries exactly twelve sectors and three rings, that every glyph stays on the canvas, that the 1962 Aquarius stellium separates strictly better than the raw ring and to at most one touching pair, and that the SVG document is well formed with sectors, rings and the Sun glyph present.
