render

The wheel as a display list, and the first picture

handbook / render

Purpose

The renderer rebuilds Robert's chart wheel from his exact geometry, the virtual canvas of 640 by 480 with the centre at 430, 224, the scale km 0.95, the rings at 90, 128, 152, 165 and 182, the polar mapping w = nb(lambda + pi − fza) that puts the ascendant on the left, the axes reaching 188 with their labels at 200. The output is a backend neutral display list, one code path can feed the screen, an SVG file or a printer later, which resolves the original's three way moda& branching by design.

Deliberate modernisations, each documented

Three parts of the original drawing cannot carry over literally, and the legacy analysis called all three. The flood fill of the sign band becomes explicit annular sector paths. His bitmap glyphs in six sizes become text glyphs, Unicode astrological symbols where they exist and his two letter tags where they do not, which realises the resolution independent vector symbols he himself prototyped in SYMBGEN. And the tiled screen dump persistence is replaced by real SVG export. The element colours stay his, fire red, earth olive, air teal, water cyan, and the aspect chords keep the colour table of aspz1 with the blue dashed node axis.

The de clumping

plentz is ported as a whole, the sort along the wheel, the radial stagger of plus eight or minus fourteen units when neighbours come within ten degrees, the angular push apart within nine degrees with his three different strengths. One honest limit is kept, the original resolves a stellium in one pass and may leave a single touching pair in extreme cases, the port behaves the same and the test asserts the improvement rather than a guarantee the original never made.

The command line front end

apps/cli builds the horcom executable. It takes a date, time in UT, place and the calculation switches, prints the coordinate table with the sign tags of his zei$ table, the houses and the aspect counts, and writes the wheel as SVG on request. The first wheel it produced cross checked against the book, the Sun of 1992-10-13 landed within seconds of arc of the Meeus example advanced by three hours of daily motion.

Verification

test_render.cpp checks that the ascendant label sits on the left horizon, that the list carries exactly twelve sectors and three rings, that every glyph stays on the canvas, that the 1962 Aquarius stellium separates strictly better than the raw ring and to at most one touching pair, and that the SVG document is well formed with sectors, rings and the Sun glyph present.

In memory of Robert Rettig, who built all of this first.
