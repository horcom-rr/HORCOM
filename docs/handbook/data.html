Purpose

The loaders that keep his files alive. Every format was specified byte level in the legacy analysis and verified against real files before a loader was written. Text arrives as Windows 1252 and is normalised to UTF-8 on read, numbers are right aligned like RSET STR$, text left aligned like LSET, and overlong text truncates exactly where his fields truncated it. He knew about that himself

The chart record

Day, month, astronomical year, hour in Universal Time, minutes with the seconds as fraction, longitude east positive, latitude north positive, then 25 bytes of name, 20 of place and 51 of remark. A remark starting with the calendar flag forces the Julian or the proleptic Gregorian calendar, the loader exposes it as the same Calendar enum the time module uses. There is no header and no index, sorting was always in memory.

Places

Eight bytes longitude, eight latitude, twenty name. The picker files carry the time zone difference in the last five name bytes, stored as the correction that leads from zone time to UT, so MEZ reads minus one. The preferred place file has two coordinate encodings in the wild, integer micro degrees from the write once writer and plain decimals from older files, the reader takes the original's branch on the missing decimal point including its kk epsilon.

The repository ships his two hand curated zone picker files as data/places/europa.int and welt.int, the Qt shell's place search reads any file in that folder plus files opened by hand, so the full SPEZ_ORT gazetteer of the archive works when copied in. A data/ort.ext in the original format seeds the startup coordinates when present.

Privacy rule

His real chart collections contain real people and never enter the repository. The loader tests run on synthetic records written on the fly, the byte layout itself was verified against non personal files, places and settings, during the analysis.

The settings stream

KONSTA7P.INT holds every calculation switch of the program. Its writer and reader split the fields across lines differently, GFA INPUT ignores line boundaries, so the loader tokenises the whole file first, quoted strings with commas inside included. The struct keeps his variable names as the schema, the mapping onto the pipeline settings follows kon_dhol with the sixteen to twelve divisor rule of the equal probability mode. Validated against his real settings file, which showed his own configuration ran with the topocentric parallax switched on. Its values live on in code as robert_profile(), verified byte for byte against the archive file through the writer, and the Qt shell starts on it, parallax on, true node and true apogee, his own orb table in the equal probability mode. A konsta file placed next to the data still overrides the profile, the historical file name included. The shell's Vorgaben dialog edits the live values, orb factor, maximal divisor, the equal probability mode, the planet weights and the fixed point, and writes them back as konsta.int beside the data on request, so the next start keeps them.

The statistics store

STATIST7 keeps one dataset as three files. The .STA carries 210 byte records, name, place, a packed clock and 34 int32 angles in radians times ten million, the .STH twin adds the six late extras under the eight characters plus one naming rule, and the .PAR parameters give the extra slots their meaning, without it a .STA cannot be read. The loader maps everything onto body slots, detects heliocentric files by the zeroed axes, and the writer packs byte for byte, pinned by a round trip. Verified locally against his real datasets, over sixteen hundred charts parse with each sun standing where its calendar date demands. The shell's Statistik browser lists a dataset, shows the sign distribution of any object over the whole set and hands a chart back to the panel.

The search conditions

stat_ausw runs conditions over a loaded dataset, ported as evaluate_statistics. The object menu covers a single body, a house cusp or the ruler of a cusp's sign, the lights, all bodies at once, the ruler of a house, a midpoint, an aspect between two operands or against a midpoint, mirror points around both axes, a name fragment and the arabic parts with their day and night formula swap. The condition menu places the object at a degree, in a sign, in a house, near another body or anywhere. Windows that cross the zero point run as extra passes exactly like his two follow up loops. haus_def rebuilds all twelve cusps from the six the record stores, ze_pl hands each sign its ruler with the alt switch to the classical ones, and the aspect windows share org, orbis_discr and the equal probability multiple filter with the aspect engine, including his sticky per record flags under UND. Conditions chain through the mz survivor mask, UND keeps only records that carried a previous match, and the lights and all bodies walks never write the mask on a match, only their failures clear it, his split kept knowingly. sum_z_h tallies every tested value into a sign or house distribution as the baseline the counts compare against. The Statistik dialog builds the conditions with UND chaining, filters the record list to the survivors and prints hit count and distribution.

The commentary texts

The KOMMEN7P folder holds Robert Rettig's own German essays, an introduction and Erläuterungen on input, the ephemeris, the chart types, solars, the Münchner Rhythmenlehre, directions, houses, statistics, the AAF world and assorted notes, plus the change log and the short manual at the program root. They are not an interpretation engine, the original had none, they are his writing shown in the TEXT-DATEI LESEN box. The texts ship with the repository in data/kommen as markdown editions, published by his family with the wording unchanged, only reflowed, with one private postal address of a third person removed and with the parts behind his mid file separators included that the original reader always stopped at. The shell renders them under the Hilfe menu. His byte exact text files still work, the ported lese_text rules read them, tilde lines and overlong lines drop, a leading dash ends the file, the bytes decode from Windows 1252, and a folder picker in the dialog opens an original KOMMEN7P folder kept anywhere else.

The AAF exchange format

The tagged text format is the port archive format going forward. The parser takes the original quirks whole, the tilde as universal ignore marker, stars as empty fields, HTML stripped so saved web pages still parse, plain lines continuing a comment, the calendar suffix on the year and the rule that a Julian date outranks the clock fields. The writer reproduces the A93 and B93 lines of make_aaf including STR$(jd,13,5). The shell writes real records through it, the record mask carries the person, the place name, the country abbreviation and the summer time code of the original sommerzeit table, the clock stays civil with the zone string beside it composed like zeitzon, and saving into an existing file can append to the collection instead of replacing it. Dateien verketten carries his chaining tool over, any mix of DAT and AAF collections reads in sequence and writes out as one AAF chain file, the way he merged collections for the statistics work.

The country tables

landnima.int names the two letter NIMA prefixes of the big place files, 263 code lines in the shape XX = NAME, and the shell's place search uses it to label the files with their countries. One documented deviation, the original discriminator starts behind its first array slot and can never match Aruba, the rewrite searches every entry. laender.int carries the 57 German auto style abbreviations of the record mask behind its header row, loaded and pinned for the record editor to come. Both ship as seeds in data.

The zone name catalogue

His zonnamen.int lists 176 named zones behind the header row, credited in the original dialog title as public domain via B. Mahl. Fixed column text, the name to column 46, the abbreviation from column 47, the last ten characters the difference from zone time to UT in the shape minus eleven h twenty three m. The sign lives only in the first character because VAL of the hour field drops it, the parser keeps exactly that arithmetic. The LMT and LTT rows carry no number, there local time follows from the longitude. The catalogue ships as data/zonnamen.int and the shell's zone picker reads it, still under his dialog title.

Verification

test_data.cpp pins the 1252 round trip including the high row, the exact field offsets and alignments of an encoded chart record, file round trips with a BC year and the Julian flag, rejection of torn files, the place layout, the zone suffix readings for east and west, and both preferred place encodings. The shipped europa picker is pinned to its 465 places with Agram at minus one, and his profile is pinned on every switch and round trips through the writer and reader unchanged. test_statist_eval.cpp runs invented charts through every object and window, the cusp mirroring, the twelve rulers with the alt switch, the zero wrap, the aspect fold with the multiple filter, the UND survivors and the mask silence of the collective walks.

In memory of Robert Rettig, who built all of this first.
