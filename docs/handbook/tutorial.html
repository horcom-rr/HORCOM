Tutorial

A first tour through the HORCOM shell

← back to the handbook

Getting started

Download the newest release from the GitHub releases page, unpack it anywhere and start horcom_gui.exe. The menu bar keeps the five columns of the original main menu, Datei for records and files, Ephemeride for the sky tables and statistics, Horoskope for the chart views, Auswertung for returns and directions, Diverses for the remaining tools. The data folder must stay beside the program, it carries the planetary tables, the ephemeris files and the place and zone catalogues. The program speaks German on a German Windows and English everywhere else, --lang de or --lang en on the command line overrides that.

The first chart

The Eingabe panel on the left is the whole input. Type a birth date and clock time, then the time zone in hours east of Greenwich, the little … button beside it opens Robert's zone name catalogue when you only know the zone by name. Longitude and latitude take decimal degrees, east and north positive, and Ort suchen in the Datei menu (Ctrl+L) finds both from his gazetteer of places instead. Choose the house system, Placidus is his default, and the chart recomputes with every change, there is no calculate button.

The switches under the fields are his classic options. Parallaxe computes topocentrically, his special pride, the positions then carry the correction of the observer standing on the Earth's surface rather than its centre. Zusatzplaneten opens the extra bodies, Chiron and the asteroids among them, where an ephemeris file reaches. Wahrer Mondknoten and Wahres Apogäum switch the node and the Black Moon from their mean to their true positions. Heliozentrisch turns the whole sky sun centred, the Earth appears on the wheel and the houses disappear, exactly as the original did it.

Reading the screen

The banner on top shows the Julian date, delta T, the sidereal time as ARMC and the active house system. The wheel lies like paper on the desk, the ascendant on the left, aspect chords in the middle, and the birth data written down the left margin of the sheet. The Koordinaten table lists every body with longitude, latitude, declination and daily motion, an R marks retrograde. The Häuser table lists the twelve cusps, and the line under the wheel counts the found aspects. The mouse wheel zooms into the drawing around the cursor, dragging with the left button pans the zoomed sheet, and a double click brings the whole wheel back.

Records

Datensätze öffnen (Ctrl+O) reads his chart collections, the original DAT files as well as the AAF exchange format. Double click a record and it fills the panel, marked records can be deleted from the file. Datensatz bearbeiten (Ctrl+D) opens the record mask with name, place and country, and Als AAF speichern (Ctrl+S) writes the current chart back into an AAF collection, so your records travel between HORCOM and every program that reads AAF. Dateien verketten merges any mix of DAT and AAF collections into one AAF chain file, AAF-Datei → HORCOM-Datei converts an exchange file into his fixed record layout, Datei trimmen/minimieren tidies a collection, and Statistik-Datei erstellen computes a whole collection into a research dataset.

The running sky

The Transite switch lays the sky of the transit moment as an outer ring over the radix, with the running aspects listed under the wheel. Transit-Liste scans a whole date range for exact transit events and Ingresse tables the sign entries of the running bodies. Uhr turns the chart into his running clock, redrawn every fifteen seconds for the present moment at the panel's place.

Returns

The Auswertung menu carries the whole return family. Solar finds the moment the transiting sun returns exactly onto its radix degree in a chosen year and draws that chart, Lunar does the same with the moon in a chosen month or, by number, counts the n-th lunar from birth for the Troinsky tertiaries. Both use his backward search, the same machinery his plant routine drove. Solar-Liste and Lunar-Liste table up to 84 return dates at once, and an ambiguous Planetar offers all its retrograde crossings to choose from.

Two people

Vergleich asks for a second record and rides it as an outer ring over the radix, with the cross aspects listed below. Composit builds the midpoint chart of both, the house mode follows his profile, and Combin averages moment and place into one chart. The 90°-Kreis shows the comparison on the 90 degree dial, everything multiplied by four so the hard aspects of both charts stand side by side, the three sign qualities colour the band.

Special charts

Direktionen turns the axes at his Naibod rate to an event date, direct or converse, with the sidereal time variation he used for rectification. Mundan switches to the horm 2 view, every body projected along its semi arc into house space. Harmonic asks for an order and lays the harmonic chart, every longitude multiplied, as an outer ring over the radix, and Multi-Direktionen does the same with the six age directed wheels of the MULTI menu. Septar, Planetar and Personar extend the returns beyond solar and lunar, and Progressions-Horoskop and Tages-Horoskop bring the day for a year clock and the chart of any day at the birth's true solar time.

Direction evaluations

Direktionen-Auswertung lists arcs as ages for the symbolic methods and the Kühr primaries, and dated events for the secondary, sun arc and moon arc directions. Rhythmenlehre walks the seven year phases of the Döbereiner school and tables every trigger, and the Dynamogramm draws the summed intensity curves after Krafft-Goerner over five years of life. The Linear-Graphik spreads directions or transits as curves over a time axis in the manner of the Ebertin life diagram, every crossing with a radix line a direction becoming exact, and the Grad-Datum-Liste maps every half degree onto its age with the Gruppenschicksals-Grade marked and own degrees definable.

Sky tables

Under Ephemeride, Fixsterne lists his sixty two stars with their aspects to the chart, Arabische Teile the thirty seven sensitive points with the day and night formulas, Grad-Liste the sorted degrees and Ingresse the sign entries. Under Diverses, Aufgang/Untergang tables the rising, meridian and setting clocks, Finsternisse the new and full moons with the eclipse letters and, on demand, their aspects to the valid record, and Großes Jahr the precession driven age point. Histogramme in the Horoskope menu counts the element and quality columns with his point weights.

The Aspektarium

Aspektarium in the Horoskope menu opens his triangular aspect matrix, exact separations above the diagonal, the named aspects below it, the hit count of every body on the diagonal, and the divisor table with angle and orb beside it. The Maximaler Teiler box rescans with eight, twelve or sixteen divisors on the spot. Halbsummen-Bäume lists every point with its place on the 45 degree dial and the midpoints landing on it, close company on the dial glows red.

Fine tuning

Vorgaben (Orbes, Fixpunkt) in the Datei menu edits the orb factor, the maximal divisor, the equal probability mode and the planet weights, and places the Fixpunkt, a chosen ecliptic degree that rides the chart as the point sp. A checkbox writes everything as konsta.int beside the data so the next start keeps it. Korrektur in the Diverses menu rectifies the birth time, pick sidereal time, MC, AC, an intermediate cusp, sun or moon, give the target value, and the panel clock shifts until the chosen quantity reaches it. The Direktionen dialog collects trial shifts in a sum memory and applies their mean to the radix on demand. Zeit-Wandern and Ort-Wandern step the chart through time or place with the Schiemenz counters summing along, and Planeten-Auswahl in the Ansicht menu shows single planets alone or red, highlights the birth ruler and picks the drawn aspect lines per divisor. The Vorgaben dialog also chooses the length mode, apparent with light time, with aberration, or true.

Statistics

Statistik in the Ephemeride menu loads one of his STATIST7 datasets, lists the charts and shows the sign distribution of any object over the whole set. Below that the search conditions of his stat_ausw world wait, pick an object, a body, a ruler, a midpoint, an aspect, a mirror point, a name fragment or an arabic part, say where it must stand, and apply. Conditions chain, UND keeps only records that survived the previous ones, and the record list filters to the hits.

Output

Horoskop als SVG writes the wheel as a vector file, Horoskop als PDF writes an A4 landscape page, and Drucken (Ctrl+P) sends whatever the wheel currently shows to a printer, transits, dial and comparison included.

Comfort

The Ansicht menu scales the text of the whole shell, Schrift größer and kleiner (Ctrl+Plus, Ctrl+Minus), remembered between runs. Under Hilfe the Original-Kommentare open Robert Rettig's own commentary texts, shipped with the program and readable like a handbook. They are his writing, from the introduction to the Erläuterungen on directions and the Münchner Rhythmenlehre, and they remain the best companion to this program.
