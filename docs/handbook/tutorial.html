Tutorial

A first tour through the HORCOM shell

← back to the handbook

Getting started

Download the newest release from the GitHub releases page. On Windows unpack the zip anywhere and start horcom_gui.exe, on Linux make the AppImage executable and start it, Installation has the details. On Windows the data folder must stay beside the program, the AppImage carries its own. It holds the planetary tables, the ephemeris files, the place and zone catalogues and Robert Rettig's historical time zone notes.

The program speaks German on a German system and English everywhere else. SPRACHE / LANGUAGE in the VIEW menu switches it permanently, effective on the next start, and --lang de or --lang en on the command line overrides both. This tutorial names every menu entry and dialog caption as the English edition shows it.

The menu bar carries the original main menu entry for entry. ABOUT HORCOM holds the commentaries, IN-OUT the records and files, EPHEMERIS the sky tables and statistics, HOROSCOPES the chart views, EVALUATION the returns and directions, MISCELLANEOUS the remaining tools. Only the VIEW menu is an addition of the rewrite.

The first chart

The Input panel on the left is the whole input, the person stands first. On top of it sit Back and Forward, the walk through the panel history. Every settled change counts as one step, a jump to a solar return, a loaded record or a slipped field alike, so Back doubles as the undo key (Alt+Left or Ctrl+Z, forward Alt+Right or Ctrl+Y). A step remembers the SATZ slot it belonged to, walking back over a slot switch makes that slot the active one again and the step lands in it like any settled edit, so no slot takes over the person of another. The Wahrer and Mittlerer choices of the node and the Black Moon come back with their step. CLEAN UP / RESET empties the history along with the data of the session. Type the given name and the name, they land in the record, on the banner and on the sheet corner of the wheel. Then a birth date as DD.MM.YYYY, years before Christ with a vC after the year like his record list showed them, and a clock time, then the time zone in hours east of Greenwich. The little … button beside it opens Robert's zone name catalogue when you only know the zone by name. Longitude and latitude take decimal degrees, east and north positive, and PLACE FILES / FIND PLACE in the IN-OUT menu (Ctrl+L) finds both from his gazetteer of places instead and carries the place name onto the sheet. ENTER PLACE into a place file beside it writes the current place into a place file of your own for later reuse. Choose the house system, Placidus is his default, and the chart recomputes with every change, there is no calculate button.

The switches under the fields are his classic options. Summer time raises the entered clock time by one hour when checked, the same shift his summer time file carried, so daylight saving hours reach UT without editing the zone. The houses dropdown carries his menu of house systems, PLACIDUS by default. Parallax (topocentric) computes the positions for an observer standing on the Earth's surface rather than at its centre, his special pride. Additional planets CH QU XE opens the astronomically real extra bodies Chiron, Quaoar and Xena, the selection of Robert's own final profile. The lunar node and the Black Moon each ride their own row with a True or Mean radio, the check turns the body on and the radio picks between the true and the mean formula. Other elements opens the planet selection, the same dialog the VIEW menu carries, from which the asteroids, the planetoids and the Hamburg factors are picked one by one. Heliocentric turns the whole sky sun centred, the Earth appears on the wheel and the houses disappear, exactly as the original did it.

Reading the screen

The banner on top shows the Julian date, delta T, the sidereal time as ARMC and the active house system. The wheel lies like paper on the desk, the ascendant on the left, aspect chords in the middle, and the birth data written down the left margin of the sheet. The planet coordinates table beside it, switched on and off under VIEW, lists every body with longitude, latitude, declination and daily motion, an R marks retrograde. EPHEMERIS offers his own PLANET COORDINATES and EXTRA PLANET COORDINATES screens with every column of the original, his box of the house cusps AC, H2, H3, MC, H11 and H12, and the VARY THE TIME walk. The house cusps table lists the twelve cusps and comes and goes with HOUSE CUSPS ON / OFF in the VIEW menu, and the line under the wheel counts the found aspects, lists the mirror points and the Moon phase. Both tables can be dragged by their yellow title to another side, laid on each other as tabs or floated as windows of their own, the title keeps its colours in every state and carries the buttons to float and to close. The mouse wheel zooms into the drawing around the cursor, dragging with the left button pans the zoomed sheet, and a double click brings the whole wheel back.

Records

DATA FILE IN-OUT (Ctrl+O) is the record hub of the original. It first asks for the working data file, an existing DAT collection, a fresh name to start one with the current record, or an AAF file whose DAT twin is then built beside it. File names are not cut to his eight DOS letters, and a new file gets the .DAT or .AAF suffix when none is typed. The bound file and its record count stay visible in the Input panel. The hub then asks one question per box, exactly like his dialog system. FETCH records first offers the bound data file or a Choose another file shortcut, the same file switch Robert built into every save and load path. For more than 45 records the sort mode box asks first whether to list them alphabetically from the first, second or third word of the name, by birthday or by date, then the chooser lists name, date, UT clock, coordinates and place in his fixed columns. Record lists and place lists sort an umlaut like its base vowel. Up to five records can be picked, each one passes through the entry and display box on its way into a free RADIX slot. Change a fetched record in that box and OK asks SAVE the record In file … ? with the answers SAVE in file …, Continue EDITING and ONLY TAKE OVER as a record. Where an AAF file stands beside the data file, his warning Make changes in the AAF file only ! Otherwise DATA may be LOST !! comes first and only CONTINUE ANYWAY leads on to the question. SHOW CHART ONLY opens the chart of the marked row in a window of its own, Space leads back to the list and F6 turns it heliocentric. After the first use the list stays a browser, the original's way of looking through a whole file without taking any record. ENTER the CURRENT record appends the panel record to the file, a record of the same name raises his overwrite question, and where the AAF twin of the file exists it is the pilot. A record of the same name there is found by his aaf_ident rule, the name in 25 capitals or the surname alone for a one letter given name like his star. It gives way to the new record at the end of the AAF file, and the DAT is rebuilt from it. A derived chart on the panel, a SOLAR, a COMBINE or a result taken as radix, carries the person's name with a moment of its own, so it joins the file beside the birth record and never replaces it.

DELETE records marks up to ten records for removal, optionally together with their AAF twins, and a full copy of the file survives as RRESERVE.DAT beside it. The box CONTINUE in the FILE … DELETING ? then offers another round, STOP deleting rebuilds the DAT from its AAF twin where one exists. TRIM the file drops records without a day or without coordinates and MINIMIZE the file collapses records sharing name and birth clock, both replace the file only when something was removed, like the original rename rule. Every file the program writes goes into a temporary file first, which then takes the name of the old one, so a crash never leaves half a file behind.

The fetched records live in the five RADIX DATA slots of the IN-OUT menu, SATZ1 to SATZ5. The checked slot is the chart on the wheel. Clicking a slot in the menu switches to it and opens its ENTRY and DISPLAY BOX like the original, OK takes an edit over and its save question writes it into the data file, CANCEL just leaves the slot active. Inside COMPOSITE, COMBINE and DOUBLE WHEEL the SATZ clicks pick the records without that box. Every computed return chart writes itself into the SOLAR... DATA row of its radix and replaces the return that stood there, so the latest solar return stays a menu click away like in the original, and the DOUBLE DATA rows jump to the composite, the combine chart and the double wheel. RESULT as RADIX in the MISCELLANEOUS menu promotes the active derived chart or the combine into a fresh RADIX slot, so transits, combines and further returns can ride on top of a solar return, a progression or any of the return family. The name stays, the slot reads like SATZ2: SOLAR 2000 ALS RADIX NAME, and the entry box of the new radix opens with that label. His warning Only for the EXPERIENCED ! CHAOS LOOMS ! comes first with CANCEL as the default. When all five RADIX slots are taken the slot with the number of the result gives way after his overwrite question, together with the SOLAR entry of that number, and Mistake leaves everything as it was. Fetching records into full slots follows the same rule with the number of the active chart. CLEAN UP / RESET asks DELETE the DATA and STORED PICTURES of this session ? and on OK empties all slots and returns to the plain wheel. Once a composite, a combine or a double wheel was made, its DOUBLE DATA row names the pair, for example DOUBLE WHEEL: NAME ONE-NAME TWO with each name cut to ten letters like the original, and a click brings that pair back without asking again. An empty DOUBLE WHEEL row asks MODE ? and starts a new comparison. DOUBLE WHEEL, 90 DEGREE DIAL, COMPOSITE and COMBINE take their records from the filled slots like his SATZ clicks.

Entering new records

CHANGE IN-OUT DEFAULTS sets the input mode of the edit fields. With AUTOMATIC ADVANCE, his default, a field typed to its full length hands the focus to the next one, a day after two digits and a year after four, while a field entered with the mouse keeps the focus. WITH TAB STOP or MOUSE leaves the moving to the user. The same walk sets the printer option at program start and the format of the hardcopy, half or full page.

NEW ENTRY of RECORDS (Ctrl+N) follows his original entry routine. When the aafdaten folder holds AAF files it first asks in which data format to enter. The AAF format loops through the AAF box and asks after each record whether to enter another one. There OK = Save writes the record into an AAF file, by default the twin of the working data file, asks before it overwrites a record of the same name and rebuilds the matching DAT file. Entering the chart type, country, summer time or zone field opens his pick list for it. In the zone list LMT takes the local time from the longitude, and LTT turns true into mean local time like the original, on its own before 1810 and after his question until 1890. The HORCOM format opens one empty mask per call, titled with the RADIX number of the slot it claims, and reads the clock as local clock time.

Ticking CET and OTHER ZONE TIMES opens his time zone box. Its difference field reads like his, zone time plus the value gives UT, so CET is -1. A click on a nearby place of the EUROPE or WORLD list takes its zone, the SINGLE and DOUBLE SUMMER TIME boxes add one or two hours to whatever zone the field holds, and the WORLD button opens the named zone catalogue. The mask then shows the zone difference. LOCAL TIME (HISTORICAL CHARTS) runs the clock on the longitude with his true or mean local time questions, STILL JULIAN ( AFTER 1582 ) sets the Julian calendar and the local clock with it, and READ TIME DETERMINATIONS shows his historical zone and summer time notes per country. Those notes are his own German texts and stay in his wording. A missing day raises DATE MISSING ! and a longitude beyond 180° Invalid geographic longitude !, both return to the mask with the typed fields kept.

Tabbing from the name into the place field opens his place menu, NEW PLACE or EDIT PLACE, the PREFERRED PLACE, PLACE FILES : FETCH - ENTER - DELETE, DELETE PREFERRED PLACE and UNDO. The preferred place is the single place of ort.ext in the data folder, when none is set yet the next place typed by hand becomes it. PLACE FILES picks a place file and offers his file menu. FETCH record takes a place into the mask, DELETE records marks up to ten places with RRESERVE.INT as the reserve and the same continue question, TRIM the file drops places without a name or without coordinates. Tabbing on from the coordinates of a new or changed place asks SAVE the PLACE ?, YES enters it into a place file, the third button makes it the preferred place. The AAF box opens its own event place menu when its place name field is entered.

OK then asks SAVE the record ? Or continue EDITING ?. SAVE picks the data file, a new name starts a new file with this record, an existing one takes it at the end and reports his byte and record count. ONLY TAKE OVER as a record, the default, only fills the slot. A record whose name already stands in a file with an AAF twin is refused with A RECORD of the SAME NAME EXISTS already ! Make CHANGES in the AAF FILE !, changes to such a record belong in the AAF file.

EDIT RECORD (Ctrl+D) opens the AAF box with the AAF fields, name, place, country and summer time code. Opened from there or from the AAF format button of the record mask, the box shows the record first. Back to HORCOM format takes its fields over, Change record opens them for editing and saving through OK = Save, and ENTER the CURRENT record (Ctrl+S) is the entry branch of the hub as a direct key. AAF is the exchange format, so your records travel between HORCOM and every program that reads AAF. CHAIN FILES in the MISCELLANEOUS menu merges any mix of DAT and AAF collections into one AAF chain file. AAF FILE < > HORCOM FILE beside it asks How to convert ?. The import turns an AAF file into its DAT twin, the export writes the AAF twin of a DAT file after his warning that this only makes sense for exchanging data with programs that do not read the HORCOM format. An existing twin is only replaced after his question, the export can take a new name instead and the DAT follows it so the pair stays a pair, and BUILD an EVALUATION FILE in the EPHEMERIS menu computes a whole collection into a research dataset.

The running sky

The Transits switch lays the sky of the transit moment as an outer ring over the radix, with the running aspects listed under the wheel. TRANSITS in the EVALUATION menu first asks the output mode, TABLE, HOROSCOPE GRAPHIC or LINEAR GRAPHIC. HOROSCOPE GRAPHIC asks CHOOSE the RUNNING FACTORS ?, the event place and the DATE-TIME ENTRY, then TIME UNIT ? (days, hours or minutes), a number and DIRECTION ? start his walk. V and R, D, H and M, plus and minus and Space steer it, the wheel follows every step, Esc leads to On with a NEW TIME UNIT ?, and the whole walk counts as one Back step. TABLE and LINEAR GRAPHIC ask his question boxes in turn. The base aspect comes first (15°, 22.5°, 30°, 45°, 60°, 90°, 180°, 360° or any other divisor, and the combined grids 30° and 45°, 60° and 45°, 60° and 90° that keep only the multiples of both angles), then which running factors take part (all of them, from Mars or from Jupiter outward, chosen ones marked red, or only chosen ones), the start and end date, whether the moon joins, and with the parallax on the event place. The radix points are the bodies with AC and MC. Following the direction defaults they also include the intermediate cusps of houses 2, 3, 5 and 6, the four cardinal points and the midpoints of every pair. Before the list opens the program asks SORT the LIST ? like the original, by time, by aspect or in the order found, and the button under the table sorts again. The result table sets his symbol before every body tag, colours hard aspects red and the sextile and trine green, inverts MA SA UR NE PL and shows the hour with minutes only where the running body is fast enough for it. The list also carries the retrograde passages, marked R, and the transits of the mean node, each exact to about a second. Like every direction table it follows the chosen calendar and SMALL SYMBOLS in TABLES ?, and under the parallax it names the event place. A long run of TRANSITS, MUNDANE ASPECTS, SECONDARY DIRECTION or SOLAR (LUNAR) ARC DIRECTION shows a progress window, CANCEL or Esc asks before stopping and keeps the list found so far. A double click opens the transit view of that moment.

MUNDANE ASPECTS first asks Output mode ? with TABLE or LINEAR GRAPHIC, then the same boxes. The table lists the exact aspects the running bodies make with each other, the positions of both beside every row. The linear graph draws the running bodies without radix lines, marks each aspect on the first body of the pair and steps on with NEXT interval. With a combined grid it offers his void of course rule for horary astrology, a body then only counts while it stays in the sign it stood in at the moment of the aspect. INGRESSES SUN-MOON-MC-AC asks for SUN, MOON, MC or AC, the other planets plus Chiron, Quaoar and Xena follow at the tester's request. The Sun takes a calendar year, the Moon and the planets a date, MC and AC a date and place in the record mask with three days side by side. Space, plus or PgDn page on and R, minus or PgUp back, the Sun by a year, the Moon 29 days on or 28 days back from the Pisces ingress of the table shown, the added planets by one orbital period but at least a year, the angles by three days. In the MC and AC tables the longitude column shows the axis computed at the found moment. CLOCK first shows the system time and asks whether it is right, then, the first time in a session, the place of the clock in his mask with the coordinates only. The clock reads UT from the system, his zone box for the system time is not needed. As long as the clock runs, a strip under the main screen shows its UT time, AC, MC and sidereal time every second. He then offers to take the running clock over as a record, though as he warned this is not recommended. Taken over it fills a RADIX slot that ticks every five seconds while it leads, otherwise his clock screen redraws the chart every fifteen seconds until ESC. While the clock record ticks, the menu entry reads => RECORD 'CLOCK' in grey, the panel shows its moment, and the record pauses while a dialog is open. Called again the clock offers to delete itself, and ESC on its first box keeps a running clock as it is.

Returns

The EVALUATION menu carries the whole return family grouped by kind. The return charts and the Munich rhythm block come first (SOLAR with its list, LUNAR with its list, PLANETAR, PERSONAR, DAY HOROSCOPE, DYNAMOGRAM, MUNICH RHYTHM THEORY, and SEPTAR beside the rhythm where it belongs), then the direction block (PROGRESSED HOROSCOPE, SECONDARY DIRECTION, SOLAR (LUNAR) ARC DIRECTION, PRIMARY DIRECTION and the symbolic directions with the DEGREE DATE LIST), then the transit block (LINEAR GRAPHIC, TRANSITS, MUNDANE ASPECTS). SOLAR finds the moment the transiting sun returns exactly onto its radix degree in a chosen year and draws that chart, and it carries a Previous year and Next year button so a childhood year can be walked without leaving the dialog. LUNAR does the same with the moon. Its box SEARCH with : offers DATE, NO. FUTURE and NO. PAST with his hints, by number it counts the n-th lunar return from birth for the Troinsky tertiaries, and Previous lunar and Next lunar step it. Number 0 is the radix itself, in PLANETAR as well. Both use his backward search, the same machinery his original routine drove. SOLAR, LUNAR and PLANETAR show the event place with a Choose place… button that opens EVENT place = BIRTH place ?. The return is searched and cast at that place while its target stays the radix position at the birth place, and the birth record keeps its own place. Every result carries the count of returns since birth, like 32.SOLAR, 668.LUNAR or 3.TERRAR, and replaces the SOLAR... DATA row of its radix, so the stepping buttons never fill further rows. SOLAR LIST and LUNAR LIST draw up to 84 return dates on one sheet, the lunar list from the last lunar before four days past the start date, and a click takes exactly the return of the clicked row over. PLANETAR searches by date or by number, then lists the passages of that return, a body in a retrograde loop crosses its radix point three times, the last row walks on to the return before. Pick a moment to view the chart or to take the time over. PERSONAR asks for the planet, then the place, shows the chart until a key and closes with CREATE a FURTHER PERSONAR ?. With the heliocentric switch on, SOLAR becomes TERRAR, the Earth's return in a chosen year. Chiron, Quaoar and Xena ride the planetar body list unconditionally so the additional planets stay available even when the panel switch is off.

Two people

DOUBLE WHEEL / 90 DEGREE DIAL asks for the mode, then for the record of the inner circle and the record of the outer circle, each one of the filled RADIX or SOLAR slots, just like his clicks on the SATZ entries. The sheet shows both coordinate columns on the left, INNER and OUTER with their house cusps, the grid of the cross aspects under them, exact ones included, and both records in the corners. The 90 degree dial multiplies the wheel by four so the hard aspects of both charts stand side by side, the columns and the grid keep the natal longitudes. COMPOSITE asks for the house system of the midpoint chart, the mean sidereal time of both, Robert Hand at the place where the pair lives or the schematic halves from the MC midpoint, then for partner one and partner two from the slots, and under Robert Hand for that residence. COMBINE takes two to five records one click after the other, asking after each whether to fetch another or to output, and averages moment and place into one chart, its place stored under the name COMBIN-ORT like in his data files. COMPOSITE and COMBINE switch Heliocentric off and carry the fixed point along.

Special charts

* PRIMARY DIRECTED AXES * in the CORRECTION box turns the axes at his Naibod rate to an event date, direct or converse, with the sidereal time variation he used for rectification. The event date box also asks a UT time, and the variation turns the axes exactly as far as the corrected radix will turn them. The wheel carries his texts, the sidereal time difference, the event moment, direct or converse, the directed sidereal time, radix or event planets and, once a variation was made, its value, running sum and mean. The REFERENCE SYSTEM of CHANGE HOROSCOPE DEFAULTS switches to his mundane frame, every body projected along its semi arc into house space, and the wheel carries Mundane in its centre. MULTIPLE DIRECTIONS / HARMONICS asks for one of his seven modes. The MULTI modes ask the event date, whether the houses move like the planets or come new from the directed MC, and for MULTI 3 and MULTI-ARC the reference factor, the FIXED POINT among them, then draw the directed chart outside the radix with both coordinate columns, the tight cross aspects and the list of directed midpoints on the axes and cusps. HARMONICS asks the order instead of a date and asks again for an order of 0. The session always draws a geocentric radix in the ecliptic frame with the mean node and the mean Black Moon, whatever the panel says, and the panel stays as it is. The MULTI modes direct the fixed point, the harmonics and the zero point modes leave it out, and the Hamburg factors never show. After a key the birth time can walk in hours, minutes or seconds while the sheet follows, and after the next key another date or order follows or the session ends. SEPTAR asks for the year of life of interest and the phase (seven months is the family's default, as in the original) and jumps to the solar return of the corresponding year. The result line names the covered life span, so the first solar return keeps its meaning of "1st SEPTAR = RADIX". PLANETAR and PERSONAR extend the returns beyond solar and lunar, and PROGRESSED HOROSCOPE and DAY HOROSCOPE bring the day for a year clock and the chart of any day at the birth's true solar time, both with Heliocentric switched off.

Direction evaluations

CHANGE DIRECTION DEFAULTS heads the EVALUATION menu like in the original. Pick a topic and it walks that one and every topic after it, EXIT ends the walk. The topics are the sextile of the Munich rhythm theory, the paging of long tables, small or large symbols, the marking of the heavy planets and the aspect colours in the tables, the intermediate cusps and the cardinal points as direction and transit targets, the latitude of significator and promissor in the primary directions, the midpoints in the tables, all of them or only those with three different factors, and the ordinate direction of the linear graphs, upward or downward after R. Ebertin. The answers are written into konsta.int with the rest of the defaults when the walk ends.

The direction block of EVALUATION carries his entries one by one. SECONDARY DIRECTION and SOLAR (LUNAR) ARC DIRECTION first ask the output mode. TABLE lists the dated directions. HOROSCOPE GRAPHIC asks CHOOSE the RUNNING FACTORS ? and Enter the SEARCH DATE !, read at the birth clock, and lays the progressed chart or the radix moved by the solar arc as an outer ring over the radix. The secondary direction also asks CALCULATION MODE ?, house rotation by one day for one year or the strictly proportional conversion, and neither asks a place. The same walk as for the transits follows, with symbolic years, months and days as further units, and HOROSCOPE GRAPHIC stays grey for the lunar arc. LINEAR GRAPHIC opens the linear graph of that kind. The linear boxes follow his a18eing with lin!, the combined grids and the small bases stay grey, MARK SINGLE … RED stays grey as well, and neither OUTPUT FORMAT ? nor the Black Moon question is asked. The transits open on the first of the month with a window of one, four or sixteen months, the directions count from a year of life over 5 to 80 years, then his orientation box, hit lines, a fixed grid or none, and whether to mark the signs, both kept with the defaults, and FINE or THICKER lines. Each date of the axis sits over its own day tick and the hit lines wear his colours. The graph carries no stationary marks, no moon hits on the four and sixteen month axes, no hits of the true node or the true Black Moon and no Part of Fortune curve for the transits and the secondary direction. After the graph NEXT and PREVIOUS interval move the window. PRIMARY DIRECTION ( E.C.KÜHR ), SYMB. DIRECTION: EQUATORIAL (with his MUNDANE or RA SYSTEM question) and SYMB. DIRECTION: ECLIPT. list arcs as dates, as years of life with the month, or as the bare arc. All of them ask the same question boxes as the transits, the output format, the base aspect, for the arc methods the key number (one year per degree, NAIBOD or any other key), which planets take part, and the years of life to count from and to. The headings of the symbolic and primary lists always show the key as Key:… Y/°, and choosing a single factor there answers CHOOSE AT LEAST 2 FACTORS ! and asks again. The secondary direction, the solar and lunar arcs, the primary and the symbolic directions in the RA SYSTEM or MUNDANE switch Heliocentric off. The secondary direction lists the retrograde passages and the mean node like the transits and asks whether to include the moon, the arc methods always take the moon along like the original. The intermediate cusps, the cardinal points and the midpoints join as the direction defaults say, the midpoints only in the ecliptic methods, and the primary list names the latitude choice of significator and promissor under the table. Every list asks SORT the LIST ? before it opens and sorts again from its button, by time or by aspect.

The MUNICH RHYTHM THEORY switches Heliocentric off and walks the seven year phases of the Döbereiner school. After a hint when the house system is not Placidus, his EVALUATION MODE box offers the GRAPHIC, one wheel per phase with the rulers of the phase inverted unless they also stand in its house, the triggers on a time axis beside it and Space to walk on, the TRIGGER TABLE and the DEGREE DATE LIST, and it defines or deletes own degrees for the list. The boxes that follow ask the direction, the date or year and month format, the beginning phase, the time unit and the period per house, then the SPECIAL POINT, kept from the last run, defined as an ecliptic degree or through a date, or none. A SEPTAR asks the same unit and period, then the year of life of interest and the special point in a box that names the Septar being cast, and names the years it governs. The DYNAMOGRAM draws the summed intensity curves after Krafft-Goerner over five years of life, and with WATCH the SINGLE ARCS ? it first shows one screen per pass of its analysis. The LINEAR GRAPHIC spreads directions or transits as curves over a time axis in the manner of the Ebertin life diagram, every crossing with a radix line a direction becoming exact, and the DEGREE DATE LIST maps every half degree onto its age with the group destiny degrees marked and own degrees definable.

Sky tables

Under EPHEMERIS, FIXED STAR POSITIONS lists his sixty two stars, always computed in APPARENT 2 whatever the defaults say, with their contacts to the chart as aspect symbols, each symbol before the body tag like his stelk, the fixed point and the axes included. Right ascension and declination stand in columns of their own, the longitudes read in degrees and minutes, an aspected star wears his red on cyan band on the name, and the footer names the person, the date, the kind of chart such as RADIX and the ephemeris mode. ARABIC PARTS ( SENS.POINTS ) tables the thirty seven sensitive points with the day and night formulas, the aspects of every point in his manner and the same cyan band on the aspected rows, the DEGREE LIST G/H the sorted degrees and the INGRESSES the sign entries. Under MISCELLANEOUS, RISE / SET tables the rising, meridian and setting clocks, ECLIPSES the new and full moons with the eclipse letters, his Sun or Moon symbol on every seventh row, and, on demand, their aspects to the valid record, and GREAT ( = PLATONIC ) YEAR the precession driven age point.

HISTOGRAMS in the VIEW menu counts the element and quality columns with his point weights. Fire, earth, air and water wear his sign colours, and cardinal, fixed and mutable carry the matching quality colours in the same key so the qualities read as a matching row (the family reads that row as mutable rather than his old mixed). The window is his point weight table, the point value row takes a digit from 0 to 9 per point, the last cell weighs every additional planet, and the two switches POINTS in the 1st HOUSE DOUBLE and 1st BIRTH RULER DOUBLE double the planets of the first house and the ruler of the ascendant sign. Whatever you set there feeds the histograms of the A4 print and is written into konsta.int when the window closes. A row without any weight for SU to MC counts each of them once, like the original did when it read such a file.

The aspectarium

ASPECTARIUM in the HOROSCOPES menu asks for the maximum divisor, eight, twelve or sixteen, and draws his triangular aspect matrix, exact separations in whole minutes above the diagonal, the named aspects below it, the hit count of every body on the diagonal, the divisor table with angle and orb beside it and the planet weights under it. MIDPOINT GRAPHIC draws a tree for every point with the pairs whose midpoint falls on it, page by page with Space, Enter or Page Down, and offers the sort by the 45 degree dial at the end. The midpoints of both screens include the fixed point when one is set. The right mouse button on the chart or on the trees opens the choice of single planets, shown red or alone with their aspects or midpoints, NORMAL OUTPUT returns to all of them.

Fine tuning

CHANGE HOROSCOPE DEFAULTS in the HOROSCOPES menu is the original's wizard of eleven topics. Pick one and the questions run on from there, R or PgUp steps back a question, WEIGHTING of the PLANET ORBS and ADD the MIDPOINT LIST ? included, EXIT ends. The topics set the reference frame, the size of the symbols, the orbs (the HORCOM count of twelve degrees over the divisor, or an own table with his button for equally probable orbs, the planet weights and the orb factor), the degrees and retrograde marks, where the chart begins, the old or new sign rulers, the colours of the ring and the histograms with every row of SET COLOURS showing its colour, the histogram weights, the counted divisors with the compact midpoint list, the line colour and style of every aspect family in the ASPECT LINES screen, where Enter or Space ends the wizard, and the colour of the outer symbols. His SAVE slow SCREENS ? still asks and notes that it has no effect in this version. With the compact list on, the summary box under the house cusps lists the midpoints of the chart after the aspects, direct, square and semisquare, as body on the midpoint of a pair. CHANGE EPHEMERIS DEFAULTS runs as a chain like the original, each answer opens the next topic, R or PgUp steps back and EXIT or ESC ends it. It chooses the length mode, apparent with light time, with aberration, or true, and places the fixed point, a chosen ecliptic degree entered by sign and degree that rides the chart as the point FP, a defined point can be kept or deleted. Like the original, every change of the defaults is written at once as konsta.int beside the data, and again when the program closes, so the next start keeps it. CORRECTION in the MISCELLANEOUS menu rectifies the birth time. Pick the sidereal time, MC, AC, an intermediate cusp, the sun or the moon, give the target value, and the panel clock shifts until the chosen quantity reaches it. A correction near midnight UT still lands on the moment nearest the birth, never a sidereal day away, and the moon takes its crossing nearest the birth. The correction switches to Placidus houses and geocentric positions while it runs and restores the house system at its end, and after a sun or moon correction the PLANET COORDINATES table opens. The directions dialog collects trial shifts in a sum memory and applies their mean to the radix on demand. TIME WANDERING and PLACE WANDERING step the chart through time or place with the Schiemenz counters summing along, every step flashing the strip at the bottom of the window. A time walk adds a single Back step to the panel history, and only when its varied time is taken over, the MULTI time walk likewise. PLANET SELECTION in the VIEW menu shows single planets alone or red and highlights the birth ruler.

Statistics

STATISTICS G/H in the EPHEMERIS menu opens his statistics hub. First create an evaluable file from a data file of the SPEZIAL folder, every record is computed with the current settings, so set them before you answer PARAMETERS SET CORRECTLY ? with YES. The run locks the program until it ends, ESC or the close button ask whether to discard it, and records whose latitude the house system cannot handle are left out and counted in the report. A dataset of any size can be evaluated. START the EVALUATION then opens the dataset and asks one condition after the other, first the object, a planet or cusp, a house ruler, a midpoint, an aspect, a mirror point, a name or letter sequence or an arabic part, then where it must stand, at a degree, in a sign, in a house, at a planet or anywhere. Further conditions join with OR, AND INCLUSIVE or AND EXCLUSIVE, and the output list shows every match on his list screen, the records that meet all AND conditions in inverse video. A search without a match shows its notice and goes straight on to the next evaluation. Click a row to view that chart or to take it over as a RADIX, press W for another condition and ESC to end.

Output

SAVE HOROSCOPE as SVG writes the wheel as a vector file in Robert Rettig's own colours and lines, the same as the program window. SAVE HOROSCOPE as PDF and PRINT (Ctrl+P) first ask his printer graphic question, DIN A5 or DIN A4, and PRINT then runs the same printer setup boxes and page geometry as the PRINTER GRAPHIC of an output. A5 is the classic sheet, the wheel with the margin table. A4 is the big upright page of the original, the wheel over his large tables, positions with velocity and the p and w marks, the aspect list with the mirror contacts, the house cusps, the midpoints in their three families and his cardinal-fixed-mutable and element columns. HISTOGRAMS for ELEMENTS in CHANGE HOROSCOPE DEFAULTS decides whether those columns count the signs only, stack the house counts on top, or stay off the page. Transits, dial and comparison print like they show.

The PRINTER OPTION decides how the outputs meet the printer, like in the original. It starts off, PRINT (Ctrl+P) and the PDF export work at any time. F8 or PRINTER OPTION ON / OFF in the IN-OUT menu switch it on for users who print most outputs, the choice is kept with the defaults. While it is on, leaving an output page with Space, Return, PgUp or PgDn first shows the small HARDCOPY ? box at the lower right, NO is preselected, YES prints the page, then any key goes on. HOROSCOPE GRAPHIC and the linear graphic first ask SCREEN, PRINTER GRAPHIC DIN A5 or DIN A4, the paired charts and the SOLAR and LUNAR tables SCREEN or DIN A5. Every print first asks PRINTER READY ? and names the paper to set up, then the system print dialog opens. Switch the option off and the charts simply stand on the main window without any of these boxes. F9 (F9 = ACTIVATE DOUBLE PRINT) collects two pictures for one page, the first press explains it and keeps the output in front, the second keeps the next one, the third offers the print, both stacked on one DIN A4 page. The PRINT button of a record list and of the explanation reader prints the rows, 53 to a page with the page number on top. SHOW / or SAVE the LAST PICTURE in the IN-OUT menu brings back the page of the last output until a key, a chart that simply stood on the main window with the printer option off included, then saves it as a picture in the bilder folder of the data folder, loads a stored one or deletes one. Before any output it still offers the stored pictures, and right after STATISTICS or TIME WANDERING it refuses once with Not for this application ! like the original.

Comfort

The VIEW menu scales the text of the whole shell, LARGER TEXT and SMALLER TEXT (Ctrl+Plus, Ctrl+Minus), remembered between runs. Under COLOURS the shell wears one of two dresses, BLACK on WHITE like the working screens of the original, the default, or NIGHT SKY, the dark theme with gold headings and the starry banner of his splash screen. The third choice there, BACKGROUND COLOURS, asks his two colours, the background of the dialogs and the passive screen behind the wheel paper, keeps them in KONSTA and lays them over the dress, and text on those colours switches to black or white so it stays readable. Choosing a dress again gives the dress its own colours back. RESET EVERYTHING… at the foot of the VIEW menu, an addition of the rewrite, asks RESET HORCOM to the STATE of its FIRST START ? and on RESET and RESTART returns the text size, the colours, the windows and the DEFAULTS of Robert Rettig's own profile, then starts the program anew. The data of the session are lost like under CLEAN UP / RESET, the records, places and own files stay, and so does the language. Under ABOUT HORCOM the INTRODUCTION = EXPLANATION 1 opens Robert Rettig's own commentary texts, shipped with the program and readable like a handbook. The English edition shows English translations of them where one exists, the German edition keeps his original wording. They are his writing, from the introduction to the commentaries on directions and the Munich rhythm theory, and they remain the best companion to this program.

The grey rows under ABOUT HORCOM are his legend of the function keys, and the keys work like his. F1 on the main screen, or the right mouse button there, opens his First aid and introduction list with the way on to the short manual, F1 inside an output opens the explanation of the menu the output came from. F2 shows the chart in between only in the outputs where the original allowed it. There a derived chart asks whether it or the radix shows, the returns and the day charts show the radix at once, and F2, Alt+A or ESC closes it again. F3 starts the calculator of the system, F5 the angle conversion, F6 switches between heliocentric and geocentric and says so, F7 copies the window in front to the clipboard, where his program handed the screen to MSPAINT, and offers to save it as a PNG picture in the bilder folder of the data folder, F8 switches the printer option and keeps it with the defaults, F9 activates the double print. The menu bar keeps HORCOM's own access letters, so Alt+H, D, C, R, F, Z and M reach the function keys of his legend from the main screen, and inside any output Alt+E, A, C, R, F, D or Z and M act like F1, F2, F3, F5, F7, F8 and F9. A chart on the main window waits for a key only where one of his questions follows it, the PLANETAR, PERSONAR and TERRAR returns before the next one and MULTI before its next date, or while the printer option offers the HARDCOPY. Then the menu greys, a bar at the foot of the chart reads ON with SPACE or a mouse click, the title shows his hints, F1 opens the matching commentary and the right mouse button picks planets. Every other chart simply stays with the menu free, and the LAST PICTURE, the HARDCOPY and the double print preview show the same bar while they wait. ESC on the main screen, the close box of the window and every other way of closing HORCOM ask his question END the PROGRAM HORCOM ?, and DESKTOP ( QUIT HORCOM ) in the MISCELLANEOUS menu carries the quit key of the system. ESC in one of his question boxes cancels the box and the flow it belongs to, the usual meaning on today's desktops, where his alert boxes took ESC as a click on the preselected button. CHANGES / NOTES / QUICK GUIDE offers his change list and the short manual, his Windows notes belonged to the historical installation and are not shipped.
