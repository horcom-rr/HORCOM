Tutorial

A first tour through the HORCOM shell

← back to the handbook

Getting started

Download the newest release from the GitHub releases page, unpack it anywhere and start horcom_gui.exe. The menu bar carries the original main menu word for word, ÜBER HORCOM for the commentaries, EIN-AUSG. for records and files, EPHEMERIDE for the sky tables and statistics, HOROSKOPE for the chart views, AUSWERTUNG for returns and directions, DIVERSES for the remaining tools, only the Ansicht menu is an addition of the rewrite. The data folder must stay beside the program, it carries the planetary tables, the ephemeris files and the place and zone catalogues. The program speaks German on a German Windows and English everywhere else. Sprache / Language in the Ansicht menu switches it permanently, effective on the next start, and --lang de or --lang en on the command line overrides both.

The first chart

The Eingabe panel on the left is the whole input, the person stands first. On top of it sit Zurück and Vor, the walk through the panel history. Every settled change counts as one step, a jump to a Solar, a loaded record or a slipped field alike, so Zurück doubles as the undo key (Alt+Links or Strg+Z, forward Alt+Rechts or Strg+Y). Type Vorname and Name, they land in the record, on the banner and on the sheet corner of the wheel. Then a birth date as TT.MM.JJJJ, years before Christ with a vC after the year like his record list showed them, and a clock time, then the time zone in hours east of Greenwich, the little … button beside it opens Robert's zone name catalogue when you only know the zone by name. Longitude and latitude take decimal degrees, east and north positive, and ORTS-DATEIEN / Ort suchen in the EIN-AUSG. menu (Ctrl+L) finds both from his gazetteer of places instead and carries the place name onto the sheet. ORT in Orts-Datei eintragen beside it writes the current place into a place file of your own for later reuse. Choose the house system, Placidus is his default, and the chart recomputes with every change, there is no calculate button.

The switches under the fields are his classic options. Sommerzeit raises the entered clock time by one hour when checked, the same shift his SOMMERZEIT-Datei carried, so daylight saving hours reach UT without editing the zone. The Häuser dropdown carries his menu of house systems, PLACIDUS by default. Parallaxe computes topocentrically, his special pride, the positions then carry the correction of the observer standing on the Earth's surface rather than its centre. Zusatz-Planeten opens the astronomically real extra bodies Chiron, Quaoar and Xena, the selection of Robert's own final profile. Mondknoten and Schwarzer Mond each ride their own row with a Wahrer or Mittlerer radio, the check turns the body on and the radio picks between the true and the mean formula. Andere Elemente opens the Planeten-Auswahl, the same dialog the Ansicht menu carries, from which the Asteroiden, die Planetoiden und die Hamburger Faktoren are picked one by one. Heliozentrisch turns the whole sky sun centred, the Earth appears on the wheel and the houses disappear, exactly as the original did it.

Reading the screen

The banner on top shows the Julian date, delta T, the sidereal time as ARMC and the active house system. The wheel lies like paper on the desk, the ascendant on the left, aspect chords in the middle, and the birth data written down the left margin of the sheet. The Koordinaten table lists every body with longitude, latitude, declination and daily motion, an R marks retrograde. The Häuser table lists the twelve cusps, and the line under the wheel counts the found aspects, lists the mirror points (Spiegelung) and the Moon phase. The mouse wheel zooms into the drawing around the cursor, dragging with the left button pans the zoomed sheet, and a double click brings the whole wheel back.

Records

Daten-Datei Ein-Ausgabe (Ctrl+O) is the record hub of the original. It first asks for the working Daten-Datei, an existing DAT collection, a fresh name to start one with the current record, or an AAF file whose DAT twin is then built beside it. The bound file and its record count stay visible in the Eingabe panel. The hub then asks one question per box, exactly like his dialog system: Datensätze HOLEN fetches records, for more than 45 records the SORTIER-MODUS box asks first whether to list them alphabetically from the first, second or third word of the name, by birthday or by date, then the chooser lists name, date, UT clock, coordinates and place in his fixed columns. Up to five records can be picked, each one passes through the EINGABE- und ANZEIGE-BOX on its way into a free RADIX slot. AKTUELLEN Datensatz EINTRAGEN appends the panel record to the file, a record of the same name raises his overwrite question, and where the AAF twin of the file exists it is the pilot, the record lands there too and the DAT is rebuilt from it. Datensätze LÖSCHEN marks up to ten records for removal, optionally together with their AAF twins, a full copy of the file survives as RRESERVE.DAT beside it. Datei TRIMMEN drops records without a day or without coordinates and Datei MINIMIEREN collapses records sharing name and birth clock, both replace the file only when something was removed, like the original rename rule.

The fetched records live in the five RADIX-DATEN slots of the EIN-AUSG. menu, SATZ1 to SATZ5, the checked slot is the chart on the wheel and clicking another slot switches to it. Every computed return chart writes itself back into a SOLAR...-DATEN slot the same way, so a Solar stays a menu click away like in the original, and the DOPPEL-DATEN rows jump to Composit, Combin and the double wheel. ERGEBNIS als RADIX in the DIVERSES menu promotes the active derived chart into a fresh RADIX slot with its origin tagged in the name, so Transite, Combin and further returns can ride on top of a Solar, a Progression or any of the return family. AUFRÄUMEN / RÜCKSETZEN empties all slots and returns to the plain wheel. DOPPEL-KREIS / VERGLEICH, 90-GRAD-KREIS, COMPOSIT and COMBIN offer the loaded slots first and open the file chooser only when the partner is not in memory yet. Neu-Eingabe von Datensätzen (Ctrl+N) runs the entry box in a loop, every new record lands in the next slot and can be entered into the bound file at the end. Datensatz bearbeiten (Ctrl+D) opens the record mask with the AAF fields, name, place, country, summer time code, and Aktuellen Datensatz eintragen (Ctrl+S) is the EINTRAGEN of the hub as a direct key. AAF is the exchange format, so your records travel between HORCOM and every program that reads AAF. DATEIEN VERKETTEN in the DIVERSES menu merges any mix of DAT and AAF collections into one AAF chain file, AAF-DATEI < > HORCOM-DATEI beside it converts an exchange file into his fixed record layout, and AUSWERTEFÄHIGE DATEI ERSTELLEN in the EPHEMERIDE menu computes a whole collection into a research dataset.

The running sky

The Transite switch lays the sky of the transit moment as an outer ring over the radix, with the running aspects listed under the wheel. Transit-Liste scans a whole date range for exact transit events and Ingresse tables the sign entries of the running bodies. UHR turns the chart into his running clock, redrawn every fifteen seconds for the present moment at the panel's place, and on request the running clock takes over a RADIX slot as a record that ticks along, his zeuhr, though as the original warned it is not recommended.

Returns

The AUSWERTUNG menu carries the whole return family in the order of his tree, SOLAR, SEPTAR, LUNAR, PLANETAR, PERSONAR. Solar finds the moment the transiting sun returns exactly onto its radix degree in a chosen year and draws that chart, Lunar does the same with the moon in a chosen month or, by number, counts the n-th lunar from birth for the Troinsky tertiaries. Both use his backward search, the same machinery his plant routine drove. Solar-Liste and Lunar-Liste table up to 84 return dates at once, and an ambiguous Planetar offers all its retrograde crossings to choose from.

Two people

Vergleich asks for a second record and rides it as an outer ring over the radix, with the cross aspects listed below. Composit builds the midpoint chart of both, the house mode follows his profile, and Combin averages moment and place into one chart. The 90°-Kreis shows the comparison on the 90 degree dial, everything multiplied by four so the hard aspects of both charts stand side by side, the three sign qualities colour the band.

Special charts

Direktionen turns the axes at his Naibod rate to an event date, direct or converse, with the sidereal time variation he used for rectification. Mundan switches to the horm 2 view, every body projected along its semi arc into house space. Harmonic asks for an order and lays the harmonic chart, every longitude multiplied, as an outer ring over the radix, and Multi-Direktionen does the same with the six age directed wheels of the MULTI menu. Septar, Planetar and Personar extend the returns beyond solar and lunar, and Progressions-Horoskop and Tages-Horoskop bring the day for a year clock and the chart of any day at the birth's true solar time.

Direction evaluations

Direktionen-Auswertung lists arcs as ages for the symbolic methods and the Kühr primaries, and dated events for the secondary, sun arc and moon arc directions. Rhythmenlehre walks the seven year phases of the Döbereiner school and tables every trigger, and the Dynamogramm draws the summed intensity curves after Krafft-Goerner over five years of life. The Linear-Graphik spreads directions or transits as curves over a time axis in the manner of the Ebertin life diagram, every crossing with a radix line a direction becoming exact, and the Grad-Datum-Liste maps every half degree onto its age with the Gruppenschicksals-Grade marked and own degrees definable.

Sky tables

Under Ephemeride, Fixsterne lists his sixty two stars with their aspects to the chart, an aspected star wears his cyan band on the name. Arabische Teile tables the thirty seven sensitive points with the day and night formulas, the aspects of every point in his stelk manner and the same cyan band on the aspected rows, Grad-Liste the sorted degrees and Ingresse the sign entries. Under Diverses, Aufgang/Untergang tables the rising, meridian and setting clocks, Finsternisse the new and full moons with the eclipse letters and, on demand, their aspects to the valid record, and Großes Jahr the precession driven age point. Histogramme in the EPHEMERIDE menu counts the element and quality columns with his point weights.

The Aspektarium

ASPEKTARIUM in the HOROSKOPE menu opens his triangular aspect matrix, exact separations above the diagonal, the named aspects below it, the hit count of every body on the diagonal, and the divisor table with angle and orb beside it. The Maximaler Teiler box rescans with eight, twelve or sixteen divisors on the spot. Halbsummen-Bäume lists every point with its place on the 45 degree dial and the midpoints landing on it, close company on the dial glows red.

Fine tuning

VORGABEN HOROSKOP ÄNDERN in the HOROSKOPE menu edits the orb factor, the maximal divisor, the equal probability mode and the planet weights, and places the Fixpunkt, a chosen ecliptic degree that rides the chart as the point FP. A checkbox writes everything as konsta.int beside the data so the next start keeps it. KORREKTUR in the DIVERSES menu rectifies the birth time, pick sidereal time, MC, AC, an intermediate cusp, sun or moon, give the target value, and the panel clock shifts until the chosen quantity reaches it. The Direktionen dialog collects trial shifts in a sum memory and applies their mean to the radix on demand. Zeit-Wandern and Ort-Wandern step the chart through time or place with the Schiemenz counters summing along, and Planeten-Auswahl in the Ansicht menu shows single planets alone or red, highlights the birth ruler and picks the drawn aspect lines per divisor. The Vorgaben dialog also chooses the length mode, apparent with light time, with aberration, or true.

Statistics

STATISTIK in the EPHEMERIDE menu loads one of his STATIST7 datasets, lists the charts and shows the sign distribution of any object over the whole set. Below that the search conditions of his stat_ausw world wait, pick an object, a body, a ruler, a midpoint, an aspect, a mirror point, a name fragment or an arabic part, say where it must stand, and apply. Conditions chain, UND keeps only records that survived the previous ones, and the record list filters to the hits.

Output

HOROSKOP als SVG SPEICHERN writes the wheel as a vector file. HOROSKOP als PDF SPEICHERN and DRUCKEN (Ctrl+P) first ask his DRUCKER-GRAPHIK question, DIN A5 or DIN A4. A5 is the classic landscape sheet, the wheel with the margin table. A4 is the big upright page of the original, the wheel over the bes_big boxes, positions with velocity and the p and w marks, the aspect list with the mirror contacts, the house cusps, the Halbsummen in their three families and the sign quality and element counts with the little bars. F9 activates the DOPPEL-AUSDRUCK, press it once to store the current sheet, set up the second horoscope, press it again and both print half size on one page like his mehrf buffers. Transits, dial and comparison print like they show.

Comfort

The Ansicht menu scales the text of the whole shell, Schrift größer and kleiner (Ctrl+Plus, Ctrl+Minus), remembered between runs. Under Farben the shell wears one of two dresses, Schwarz auf Weiß like the working screens of the original, the default, or Nachthimmel, the dark theme with gold headings and the starry banner of his splash screen. Under Hilfe the Original-Kommentare open Robert Rettig's own commentary texts, shipped with the program and readable like a handbook. They stay in his German wording on purpose, also in the English version. They are his writing, from the introduction to the Erläuterungen on directions and the Münchner Rhythmenlehre, and they remain the best companion to this program.
